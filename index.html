Frequently Asked Questions

Project kitne din mein complete ho jata hai? ▼

Ye project ki complexity par depend karta hai. Simple website 2-4 weeks mein, medium projects 1-3 months mein, aur complex applications 3-6+ months mein complete hote hain. Hum initial consultation mein exact timeline dete hain.

SEO results kitne din baad nazar aate hain? ▼

SEO ek long-term strategy hai. Usually 3-6 months mein positive results nazar aane lagते hain, lekin 6-12 months mein significant improvement dekha ja sakta hai. Har industry aur competition level different hota hai.

Aap ki pricing model kya hai? ▼

Hum flexible pricing models offer karte hain - project-based, monthly retainer, aur time & material. Project requirements ko dekh kar hum customized quote dete hain. Budget ke according hum solutions tailor karte hain.

Kya aap post-launch support dete ho? ▼

Bilkul! Hum 24/7 support, regular maintenance, security updates, aur technical assistance provide karte hain. Monthly, quarterly ya yearly packages available hain depending on aapki needs.

Kya aap NDA aur confidentiality agreement sign karte ho? ▼

Haan, hum har client ke saath NDA aur confidentiality agreement sign karte hain. Aapki business information aur code 100% secure aur confidential rahti hai. Ye hamari standard practice hai.
